Welcome to PALAWAN!

Description of the Place

Palawan is a province in the western Philippines known for its beautiful beaches, clear blue waters, limestone cliffs, and rich marine life. It is one of the country's most popular tourist destinations.

History of Palawan

The history of Palawan reflects the development of its communities, culture, and natural heritage through the years. Known as one of the oldest inhabited areas in the Philippines.

Top Attractions

  • Puerto Princesa Underground River
  • Big Lagoon and Secret Lagoon
  • Kayangan Lake
  • Nacpan & Calitang Twin Beaches
  • WWII Japanese Shipwrecks
